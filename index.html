Sweet Waits privacy policy Updated October 3, 2026. Package: com.sweetwaits.sweet_waits. This page matches the policy inside the app (My Countdowns → Privacy policy). Keep them in sync. On this phone Sweet Waits has no account and no server of ours. Countdown names, dates, cup choices, sticker positions, notes, and photos stay on this device. We do not sell them. We do not upload them. Photos A Memory Jar holds up to 6 photos you pick from your gallery or take with the camera. The app only gets the photos you choose. Deleting the countdown deletes those photos from the app. Uninstalling the app deletes what it stored. Reminder Daily pearl ping is optional and stays on this phone. If you turn it on, the phone schedules a notification around 9:00 that says today's pearl is waiting. The quote is not in the notification. Turn it off on My Countdowns. Share Share hands a postcard to your phone's share sheet. We do not see which app you send it to. Fonts Type on the screen is loaded from Google Fonts. That request goes to Google and can include your IP address. We do not receive it. Google's privacy policy covers that request: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Kids Kids can use Sweet Waits. We do not ask for an email or an account. Do not put a child's full name, school, address, or a photo you would not want on this phone into a countdown if other people use the phone. Ads No ads. No analytics. No tracking. Changes If this policy changes, the date at the top changes, and the copy inside the app changes with it. Contact Questions: use the contact on the Sweet Waits page on Google Play.
